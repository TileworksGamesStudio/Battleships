MISSION BRIEFING // PROTOCOL 104

1. FLEET DEPLOYMENT

Drag your 5 combat vessels into your grid or click on them in the dock and tap your sector to place. Use Rotate (or press R / Space) to alter ship axis. Ships must remain within sector coordinates and cannot overlap.

2. OFFENSIVE ENGAGEMENT

Once deployed, tap any coordinate cell in Hostile Waters to launch artillery strikes. A green radar pip denotes a Miss; a crimson fireball denotes a direct Hit. When all segments of a warship are destroyed, the vessel sinks.

3. TACTICAL COMMAND WARFARE

Landing hits and exchanging fire accumulates Command Energy. Use it to unleash special combat options:

  • Sonar Ping (35 CP): Reveals the total number of hostile vessel segments inside a designated 3×3 zone.
  • Air Recon (60 CP): Reveals coordinates along an entire horizontal & vertical crosshair.

4. ADVERSARY INTEL (AI)

Choose your opponent's battle capability from the top selector: Ensign for classic tactical gameplay, Captain for aggressive hunter-seeker strikes, or Fleet Admiral for high-tier real-time Probability Density Matrix targeting.
