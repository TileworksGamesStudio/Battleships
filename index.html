MISSION BRIEFING // AEGIS PROTOCOL

1. MOBILE CONTROLS & THUMB OPERATION

Use the upper Offensive / Defensive tabs to flip boards with one hand. Tap an enemy sector once to lock target coordinates, and tap FIRE SALVO (or double tap) to launch.

2. FLEET DEPLOYMENT

Position your 5 warships by tapping the dock card and placing it on your friendly harbor grid. Rotate with the ROT button or press R / Space.

3. TACTICAL COMMAND ABILITIES

Hitting hostile craft builds Command Energy (CP):

  • Sonar Ping (30 CP): Reveals total ship segments within a 3×3 perimeter.
  • Cluster Barrage (50 CP): Bombards 3 cells at once.
  • Smoke Screen (40 CP): Cloaks a 2×2 friendly area against enemy sensors.
  • Air Recon (70 CP): Sweeps an entire row and column for contacts.

4. ADVERSARY INTEL

Configure enemy AI capability via the selector: from baseline Ensign patrol to mathematical Fleet Admiral running probability density matrix analysis.
